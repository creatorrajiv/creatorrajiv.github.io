Privacy Policy – Kings Pipeline

Kings Pipeline is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the YouTube channels it manages.

What it accesses: through the YouTube Data API, it uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, manages playlists and comments, and reads basic statistics for the owner's own channels.

Where data is kept: sign-in tokens and video files stay on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google user data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
